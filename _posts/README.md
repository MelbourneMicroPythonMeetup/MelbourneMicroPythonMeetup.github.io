# Notes

## Meetup recordings

Recordings are published on the [official MicroPython YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@MicroPythonOfficial/videos).
When recordings become available, add the news roundup and any separately published
speaker talks near the top of the corresponding meetup post. Match the meetup
month/year and talk details, not just the upload date; some videos are published later.

Use the existing 560×315 YouTube iframe format with a descriptive `title`,
`referrerpolicy="strict-origin-when-cross-origin"` and `allowfullscreen`.
Use a clean `https://www.youtube.com/embed/VIDEO_ID` URL (no sharing parameters).
For posts without an existing video section, add `## Recordings` after the
introduction, with a `###` heading for each video when there are multiple recordings.
Do not duplicate existing embeds or confuse videos mentioned in the news with
recordings of the meetup itself.
